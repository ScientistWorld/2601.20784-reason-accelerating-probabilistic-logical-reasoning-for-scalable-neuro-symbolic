# Research Direction

Future work should remain in the paper's problem family: compact, fidelity-preserving compilation and optimization of symbolic and probabilistic reasoning DAGs for efficient execution.

Reasonable directions include better graph-compression criteria, tighter likelihood-loss bounds, bounded-fan-in normalization, scheduling-aware graph layout, or additional graph transformations that preserve semantics. Switching to a larger neural model, using extra data, or bypassing graph reasoning is outside scope.
